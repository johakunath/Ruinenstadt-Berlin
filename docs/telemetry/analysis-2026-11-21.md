- **CPU, not pixels:** frame time ≈ render CPU + a few ms on every row. Leistung lowered the resolution to 0.6 and gained nothing (25 fps against 26 to 28 on Ausgewogen): the blur and shimmer Johannes saw. Fixed: dynamic resolution holds when the CPU alone fills the frame; Leistung now saves CPU (no outline pre-pass, shadows every third frame) instead of pixels.
- **Enemy count drives it:** tunnels (10 to 13 alive, about 500 calls) run 45 to 55 fps on the same tablet, districts with 25 to 38 alive and 650 to 825 calls run 25 to 31. The wave change of 2026-11-21 (fewer, tougher enemies from tier 10) also lowers this.
- **PC regression:** the PC ran Köpenick at 59 to 60 fps on 16 Nov (333 to 495 calls) and at 29 to 37 on 19 Nov (up to 687 calls), Xhain at 20 to 42 with 24 to 28 ms render CPU. A desktop CPU at 24 ms per frame points to per-object CPU work that grew between 16 and 19 Nov (new enemies and props, contour masks, culling). Needs a profile on the PC (Firefox profiler, one Xhain fight); not done here.

## Difficulty

- **No real deaths:** one PC death at fight 1 (two shots; fixed on 20 Nov), otherwise every run won or quit by choice. Rests: 0 to 2 per run.
- **Life pool:** the tablet hero had 755 to 951 max life in Xhain, U8 and Alexanderplatz (PC 274 to 348). Fights cost 25 to 65 % of it in Alexanderplatz; one Xhain fight took 874 damage against 423 max life and ended at 284: healing (life per kill, regen, meals) carries the hero through.
- **Fight length is not short:** 35 to 85 s per district fight, longer than the bot's 40 s, because of the number of enemies (Xhain: 431 kills in 7 fights). Johannes kills each in two hits, so the time goes into the count. Hence fewer, tougher enemies rather than more life on all of them.
- **Tunnels:** S3 in 10 min, U8 in 6 min, no rests, low damage.
