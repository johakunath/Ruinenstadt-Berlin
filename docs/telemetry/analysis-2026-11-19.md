# Telemetry analysis (backup 2026-11-19)

Source: `backup-2026-11-19.json` (95 sheet rows). Fields: `src/telemetry.ts`. Bot numbers: `docs/balance.md`. Budgets: `docs/plan-3d.md` › Render budget. Short version: the data is too thin for balance changes, but it shows clear telemetry gaps and two performance misses against the budget.

## Data quality

| | Rows | After cleaning | Players (ids) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3D (`3d-0.1.0`, 16 to 18 Nov) | 25 | 24 (1 `dup`) | 4 |
| 2D (0.40 to 0.101, 7 to 15 Nov) | 70 | 36 after the `spieler` + fields dedupe; 29 by content | 6 (5 by content) |

- **2D:** id `65ac…` holds the same 7 runs as `df2s…` (identical fields, other id), plus one 7 s start. That looks like one player whose id was reset and whose queue was sent again. I count both ids as one player below.
- **3D noise:** 13 of 24 runs are test starts or reloads: 11 quits at encounter 0 after 6 to 16 s with 0 kills, plus 2 checkpoint loads (`startAt` 1 and 5) quit after 15 and 26 s. 8 of the 13 come from the PC on 16 Nov between 22:17 and 23:51 (a dev session); the rest are 2 on the phone, 1 on the Tab S9, 1 PC checkpoint load on 17 Nov and 1 desktop Linux start. That leaves **11 real runs**: 6 on the PC, 1 on a phone, 4 on the Tab S9.
- **Version:** every 3D row says `3d-0.1.0`, so builds before and after the balance pass of 17 Nov cannot be told apart. I split by date: the PC runs (16 Nov) used the old numbers, the Tab S9 runs (18 Nov) the new ones.
- **Missing fields:** no row has `death`, or `p95`, `calls`, `alive` per encounter. Those fields came in with commit e1b85d1 (18 Nov 20:46), and the last Tab S9 run (22:47) still lacks them, so it probably played a build that was not yet deployed.
- **Damage has no scale:** `dmg` is absolute. Base life is 100 (`PLAYER.maxHp`), but gear, upgrades and meals raise it (`src/sim/stats.ts`), and healing during a fight lets damage go past max life. The bot table uses % of max life, so I cannot compare the two directly.
- **Kills** carry over on checkpoint loads (111 kills in 26 s at `startAt` 5), so kills per run are not comparable.

The PC run was two days older, though, so new content (M11c props, dinos) may account for the difference. With one version string I cannot separate the two.

## Findings (2D, only what transfers)

- **Newcomers die early.** Besides Johannes, 4 players: one won Köpenick (touch, 15.5 min); 2 died in Köpenick at waves 3 and 7 (power 42 → 47 and 42 → 67); 1 quit at wave 2. That makes 2 of 4 deaths in the first district before wave 8, which supports B5 (easier start) for the 3D game, where no newcomer has played yet.
- **Johannes' wall:** 3 deaths in a row in Tempelhof (2D district 4) at waves 2 to 3, within 27 to 60 s, with power 3337 and about 1000 to 1200 damage each (v0.87). This is the 2D form of the 3D chain problem in `balance.md` (district tiers against carried power, P33), from the other side: there the next district was a wall, in 3D the later districts are too easy.
- 2D waves took 15 to 131 s, mostly 30 to 60 s: close to the bot's 3D fights (27 to 44 s).

## Suggestions

Johannes agreed to all five (2026-11-19). 1 to 3 are built in the same PR (`EncounterLog.hp`/`low`, `v` = `3d-0.1.0+<commit>`, `test: 1`).

1. **Log max life and the lowest life per fight** (`maxHp`, `low` in `EncounterLog`), so damage reads as % like `balance.md` and the bot can be fitted (B9). File: `src/telemetry.ts`. Done.
2. **Stamp the build into `v`** (for example `3d-0.1.0+<short sha>` through a Vite `define`), so the 17 Nov pass and later changes can be separated. Files: `src/telemetry.ts`, `vite.config.ts`. Done.
3. **Mark test starts:** keep the 5 s rule, but add `test: 1` when no encounter started and the run lasted under 20 s (or drop such runs). 13 of 24 rows were noise. File: `src/telemetry.ts`. Done.
4. **Tab S9 fps on Leistung:** 29 to 44 fps in late Köpenick and Xhain is under the 45 fps floor. The open rows 1 and 11 in `docs/tasks/pt-hardware-checks.md` (heat step 2, Xhain track field) are the right place to measure it. Take the draw call peaks (666 to 675) into P1b or Q1 (lowest Leistung level), not into a new row.
5. **Do not tune balance from this data.** Use it as a first data point for P34 (touch is harder) and wait for the P33 balance session and a few runs with suggestions 1 to 3 in place. If those still show 0.5 or more life bars per normal fight on touch, give the bot a separate touch skill (B9).
