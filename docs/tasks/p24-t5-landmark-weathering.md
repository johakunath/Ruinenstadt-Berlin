# P24 landmark weathering and T5 Köpenick materials [Codex]

Status: first paint and geometry pass integrated. Review and hardware checks open.

- T5 adds slate, Rathenow brick, cream Baroque plaster, Forum granite and tinted fibreglass skins. The Rathaus, Schloss, S-Bahnhof, Forum and Spreepark statues keep their existing meshes and procedural fallbacks.
- P24 runs on named sights in Köpenick, Xhain, Alexanderplatz and Mitte. Weathering follows existing facade boxes and windows. Sculptural landmarks get a lighter treatment. The same atlas supplies soot, cracks, moss and low graffiti.
- Selected existing panes are dark, broken or boarded. Scaffolding and torn tarps appear at the Rathaus, Ostbahnhof, Palast and Reichstag where an eligible wall face exists. Small trim breaks on selected buildings keep the named silhouettes readable.
- Rubble is low decoration. Landmark colliders, route data, light pools and cutaway rules are preserved. Decals and props merge with the chunk and follow its cutaway.
- The five T5 maps and one wear atlas total 769 kB. The atlas is shared across all four districts. [Visual proof](../proof/landmark-weathering/README.md) and [hardware checks](pt-hardware-checks.md) cover the rollout.
